Internals: arithmetic

The internal functions and types of the products, the factorizations and the executors running them.

HMatrices.LeafOperator — Type
struct LeafOperator{T,M,V,S} <: AbstractMatrix{T}

The operator

    L = A * B' + α * ∑ᵢ Xᵢ * Yᵢ

known through its rows and columns, or its products with blocks of vectors, where (A, B) are low-rank factors (of type M), (Xᵢ, Yᵢ) are pairs of hierarchical matrices (of type V), and α is a scalar. It is the value of an admissible leaf with its deferred updates, which a compressor compresses once (see _flush_low_rank_leaf!): compressor(L, axes(L)..., buf), through getblock! (see PartialACA), or mul! (see RandomizedSVD).

LeafOperator{T}(A, B, pairs, α, buffer = T[], rkbuffer = T[])

The scratch vectors buffer and rkbuffer grow as needed, and can be reused (e.g. taken from the workspace of a flush).

HMatrices.hmul! — Method
hmul!(C::HMatrix, A, B, a, b, compressor; executor = SerialExecutor())

Similar to mul! : compute C <-- A*B*a + C*b, where A,B,C are hierarchical matrices (A and B possibly adjoints, C possibly a Hermitian, of which only the stored triangle is updated). Every admissible leaf of C is compressed once, with compressor, from its data and all the products which reach it (see LazyDriver), the numeric work being run by executor.

The rows of A and C, the columns of A and the rows of B, and the columns of B and C must be clustered in the same way (e.g. built from the same points); otherwise an ArgumentError is thrown.

HMatrices.LazyDriver — Type
struct LazyDriver

State of an algorithm on an HMatrix where every update is deferred until the block it targets is factorized, solved for, or flushed (e.g. the lu! and cholesky! factorizations, hmul!), with its numeric work run by an executor (SerialExecutor or ThreadsExecutor).

By the block Crout identity, every block of the factors is determined by a single Schur complement, a sum of products of blocks of the factors that precede it. Instead of evaluating these products one at a time and recompressing after each one, each node carries the products it still owes, and the exact low-rank terms already evaluated for it (see PendingUpdates), representing the update C ← C + Σₖ AₖBₖ' + α*Σᵢ XᵢYᵢ. A node is flushed right before it is factorized or solved for (see flush!), so that every admissible leaf is compressed once, with comp. This is the accumulator of Börm (2019, "Hierarchical matrix arithmetic with accumulated updates"), except that an admissible leaf is compressed once from the whole sum.

The driver does the structural work (pushing and splitting products, restricting low-rank terms) itself, and hands the numeric work to executor: the low-rank terms of a node, the flush of a leaf, and the factorization of a diagonal leaf or the triangular solve on a leaf. A leaf is written by at most one flush followed by one factorization or solve, a low-rank term by one piece of work, and a block is only read once final, which is the access pattern of the executors. The workspaces of the pieces of work are taken from buffers.

With uplo = 'U' (or 'L'), only the upper (or lower) triangle of the diagonal blocks (whose rows and columns have the same indices) is updated, as for the data of a Hermitian; uplo = 'N' updates every block.

LazyDriver(C::HMatrix, X, Y, comp; α = -1, uplo = 'N', executor = SerialExecutor())

A driver for the nodes of C (and of any HMatrix of the same type), with products of blocks of the types of X and Y (HMatrix or adjoint).

HMatrices.LazyLowRankTerm — Type
mutable struct LazyLowRankTerm{T}

An exact low-rank term A*B' of the size of the block it was computed for: the product α*X*Y of two blocks of the factors, one of them being a leaf. One of A and B is usually a factor of this leaf (not a copy), the other one is computed.

It is computed by a piece of work (see _compute_low_rank_term), and read by the flushes of the leaves below the block (see LazyDriver), through restrictions (CarryTerm).

HMatrices.UpdateBuffer — Type
struct UpdateBuffer{B,T}

Workspace of a flush: the buffer comp of the compressor, the storage lowrank of the packed low-rank factors of the leaf being compressed (see _flush_low_rank_leaf!), and two vectors scratch and rkscratch for intermediate products (those of a LeafOperator). HMatrices._compute_low_rank_term — Function
_compute_low_rank_term(X, Y, α[, buf])

Return factors (A, B) (matrices) such that α*X*Y = A*B', where X or Y (an HMatrix or its adjoint) is a low-rank leaf, or both are dense leaves (see _has_low_rank_form). The factorization is exact: its rank (see _low_rank_term_rank) is either the smallest rank of the low-rank factors, or the size of the cluster shared by X and Y, which is a leaf when X*Y targets an internal node. A factor of a low-rank leaf, or the dense data of X, is shared, not copied.

HMatrices._flush_low_rank_leaf! — Method
_flush_low_rank_leaf!(C::HMatrix, updates::PendingUpdates, α, comp, buf::UpdateBuffer)

Compress data(C) + Σ AₖBₖ' + α*Σ XᵢYᵢ for the admissible leaf C, with the carry (Aₖ, Bₖ) and the pending products (Xᵢ, Yᵢ).

The low-rank terms (the data, the carry, and the products with an exact low-rank form, evaluated here) are packed side by side into a single pair of factors (A, B) stored in buf.lowrank, so that the compressor samples them with one matrix-vector product, without allocating. The other products are only sampled by the compressor, through a LeafOperator.

HMatrices.flush! — Method
flush!(d::LazyDriver, C::HMatrix)

Apply the updates pending at C (not those pending at its descendants):

  • internal node: a pending product with a leaf factor has an exact low-rank form, which is computed once, here, and shared by the leaves below (carry); the other products are split into the products of the children; the carry is restricted to the children;
  • dense leaf: the carry and the products are added exactly;
  • admissible leaf: its data, its carry and the products with an exact low-rank form are written side by side as A*B', and A*B' + α*Σ XᵢYᵢ (the other products) is compressed once (see LeafOperator).
